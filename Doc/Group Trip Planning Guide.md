# Group Trip Planning Guide

## Plan a shared trip

Use this guide to plan a realistic group trip for three to six travelers.
It brings the main Syncinary features together in a natural order:

1. create accounts and complete onboarding;
2. create or join a group;
3. save individual travel preferences;
4. search for airports, flights, and hotels;
5. build a shared itinerary;
6. add and split trip costs; and
7. review the finished plan together.

The example below uses four travelers. For a group of three, omit one
participant. For a group of five or six, invite the additional participants
and include them in the relevant cost splits.

## Example trip

**Trip:** Austin food, music, and outdoor weekend  
**Dates:** Friday, November 13, 2026 through Monday, November 16, 2026  
**Origin:** Indianapolis (IND)  
**Destination:** Austin (AUS)  
**Group size:** Four travelers  
**Purpose:** Compare travel options, choose a hotel, coordinate activities, and
make shared costs visible before booking.

### Group roles

| Traveler | Role | Contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trip organizer | Group creator/admin | Starts the group, invites the others, and adds the first flight |
| Traveler 2 | Member | Adds a hotel and checks the shared schedule |
| Traveler 3 | Member | Adds an activity and records a shared meal |
| Traveler 4 | Member | Records an airport transfer and reviews the cost split |

No one needs to make a real booking or enter payment information. Search
results, links, and displayed prices are for planning purposes.

## Before you begin

- Use an email address for each traveler that can receive account
  verification email.
- Open [syncinary.com](https://syncinary.com) in a browser.
- Choose a new group name such as `Austin Weekend 2026`.
- Have the group organizer's invite code available to share, or use the
  invitation flow provided in the app.
- If live flight or hotel data is temporarily unavailable, continue with the
  group, preferences, schedule, and expenses using clearly labeled sample
  entries. Do not treat an empty search as a selected travel option.

## Step-by-step plan

### 1. Create accounts and complete onboarding

1. The trip organizer signs up with a display name and verifies the email.
2. The trip organizer signs in and advances through all onboarding slides.
3. The other travelers each create and verify their own accounts, then sign in
   on separate browser sessions or devices.
4. Each traveler confirms that their session opens their own groups and does
   not expose another traveler's account data.

### 2. Create and join the shared group

1. The trip organizer creates `Austin Weekend 2026`.
2. The organizer opens the group and shares the invite code or sends
   invitations.
3. The other travelers join using the available join flow.
4. Everyone reopens the group and confirms that the member list shows all
   travelers.
5. The organizer confirms the admin label. A member confirms that they are
   shown as a member rather than an admin.
6. The organizer renames the group to `Austin Weekend Trip`, and everyone
   verifies that the new name appears.

### 3. Save individual preferences

Each traveler opens **Set Preferences** and saves choices that can guide
planning:

- Trip organizer: moderate budget, food, and live music;
- Traveler 2: hotel budget limit and walkable neighborhoods;
- Traveler 3: outdoor activities and vegetarian food;
- Traveler 4: lowest-cost travel and flexible activity times.

At least one traveler should leave an optional preference blank, then reopen
the page to confirm that saved values persist without changing the other
fields.

### 4. Search for flights and hotels

The group searches together and compares the available options.

1. Open the itinerary search and select **Indianapolis (IND)** as the origin.
2. Select **Austin (AUS)** as the destination.
3. Set departure to November 13, 2026 and return to November 16, 2026.
4. Set the traveler count to four.
5. Search for flights. Compare at least two results by airline, duration, and
   displayed price. Open a booking or travel link without completing a
   purchase.
6. Repeat the same route and dates with **Hotels** selected. Compare at least
   two results by name, rating, and nightly price.
7. Discuss which flight and hotel the group would choose and why.

If a search displays an error or no results, confirm that the message is clear,
then continue planning with a labeled sample entry. The rest of the trip plan
should remain usable.

### 5. Review recommendations

After preferences and searches are complete, review suggestions for the Austin
weekend if recommendations are available. Compare them with the group's
interests and budget.

Look for practical suggestions such as a food tour, live music, or an outdoor
activity. If recommendations are unavailable, choose activities manually and
continue with the itinerary.

### 6. Build the shared itinerary

From the group detail page, open **Plan Itinerary** and add these entries:

| Date | Type | Entry | Details | Added by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nov 13 | Flight | IND to AUS | Selected outbound flight | Trip organizer |
| Nov 13 | Hotel | Austin hotel | Selected hotel and check-in | Traveler 2 |
| Nov 14 | Activity | South Congress food walk | Vegetarian-friendly options | Traveler 3 |
| Nov 15 | Activity | Live music evening | Meet at 7:00 PM | Traveler 3 |
| Nov 16 | Flight | AUS to IND | Selected return flight | Trip organizer |

1. Confirm that each entry appears on the correct day and identifies who
   added it.
2. Have Traveler 2 edit the hotel details and move the food walk to another
   day.
3. Have Traveler 4 remove the live music entry, then add it back with
   corrected details.
4. Have everyone leave and reopen the schedule to confirm that changes are
   shared.
5. If map or advisor areas are shown as unavailable, use the labeled
   alternatives instead of treating them as completed features.

### 7. Track and split costs

Open **Track Costs** and add these expenses:

| Expense | Amount | Split with |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Hotel deposit | $480.00 | All four travelers |
| Group dinner | $160.00 | All four travelers |
| Airport transfer | $64.00 | All four travelers |
| Personal coffee | $12.00 | Traveler 3 only |

1. Confirm that the total is **$716.00**.
2. Confirm that shared items are divided across the selected travelers and the
   personal coffee remains assigned only to Traveler 3.
3. If the group has five or six travelers, add them to a selected split and
   confirm that only that expense changes.
4. Have the organizer delete the personal coffee entry and confirm that the
   total decreases to **$704.00**.
5. Open the page as a member and confirm that the shared totals remain visible.

### 8. Review settings and account access

1. Open the settings panel from a search or results screen and confirm that the
   current planning context remains available.
2. Sign out of the organizer account and sign in as a member.
3. Confirm that the member sees the same group, schedule, and expenses while
   account-specific preferences remain associated with the correct account.
4. Sign back in as the organizer and confirm that the shared trip remains
   intact.

## Finished trip checklist

The group is ready to use the plan when:

- every traveler can sign in and reach the application;
- all travelers belong to the same group with the correct roles;
- individual preferences save and reload for the correct account;
- the group has compared flight and hotel options or recorded a clear
  unavailable-search message;
- the shared itinerary contains at least five dated entries;
- entries can be added, edited, removed, and viewed by the group;
- expenses show the correct total and selected-member splits; and
- signing out and back in does not remove shared trip data.
